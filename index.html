Problems We Solve

Signs your property needs attention now, not later.

Water, freeze-thaw cycles and normal structural movement wear on brick, mortar, concrete and foundations over time. Catching it early is almost always cheaper than waiting.

01

Water Getting Into a Basement or Wall

Damp spots, staining or active leaks after rain usually trace back to a specific crack, joint or exterior transition that has failed.

02

Cracked or Crumbling Brick & Stone

Loose units, spalling and stair-step cracking are signs the masonry is losing its ability to shed water.

03

Open or Missing Mortar Joints

Powdery, recessed or missing mortar lets moisture travel deeper into the wall assembly with every rain.

04

Foundation & Concrete Wall Cracks

Vertical, horizontal or widening cracks can be cosmetic — or an early warning sign worth evaluating in person.

05

Freeze-Thaw Damage

Southwest Missouri's freeze-thaw cycles expand trapped moisture and accelerate cracking in concrete and masonry.

06

Deteriorating Steps, Porches & Flatwork

Spalled, pitted or uneven concrete is both a curb-appeal issue and a trip hazard worth addressing.
