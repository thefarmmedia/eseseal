Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026. This page explains what information ESE Seal collects through eseseal.com and how it is used.

Information we collect

When you submit the free estimate form on this site, ESE Seal collects the information you provide, which may include your name, phone number, email address, property city or ZIP code, the service you are asking about, and any project details you share.

How we use it

This information is used only to respond to your estimate request and to communicate with you about your project. ESE Seal does not sell your information, and does not share it with third parties except the form-processing service (Netlify Forms) that delivers your submission to Eric.

Cookies & analytics

This site does not use advertising cookies or third-party trackers. Basic, privacy-respecting web analytics may be used to understand overall site traffic; this data is not tied to your identity.

Your choices

If you would like ESE Seal to delete information you submitted, email esenlow@esesealweatherproofing.com or call (417) 350-8848 and Eric will take care of it.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to esenlow@esesealweatherproofing.com or (417) 350-8848.
