What ESE Seal looks for during a Humansville estimate

The first step is understanding what changed. Eric looks at the location and direction of cracks, the condition of nearby mortar joints, signs of previous repairs, visible moisture, loose masonry and the way the surrounding surface drains. That helps separate a cosmetic issue from a repair that needs to address water or movement.

Cracks & separationWhere the crack starts, how it runs and whether the surrounding material is still solid.
Mortar conditionOpen, recessed, powdering or missing joints that can allow deeper moisture exposure.
Water entryLeaks after rain, damp basement areas and exterior transitions where sealant or masonry has failed.
RepairabilityWhether a focused repair can preserve the existing structure instead of replacing more material than necessary.
