Owner of ESE Seal and a masonry restoration specialist with more than 25 years of trade experience.

Owner · Eric Enlow

Old-school trade knowledge with straightforward service.

Eric Enlow founded ESE Seal after decades working in masonry restoration and weatherproofing. Public customer reviews describe him as union-taught, knowledgeable, responsive and willing to explain the repair instead of pushing unnecessary work.

That approach is the center of the company: understand what failed, explain the options, repair what can be repaired and take pride in how the finished work looks.
